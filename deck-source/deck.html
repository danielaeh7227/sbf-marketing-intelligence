01Background

SBF's promise is to mobilise business and magnify opportunities.

34,160

member companies across every sector

736 joined by choice and expect to see the value.

Mobilise Business

Bring the right members into the right rooms.

  • Market briefings, missions and dialogues (MICE)
  • Networks such as SG-WEN and YBLN, and member nights
  • Policy consultations such as the Budget survey

Magnify Opportunities

Make every member's next opportunity visible to them.

  • BizSpoke, the member newsletter
  • Grants, accreditation and renewals that matter to their business
  • Member stories on LinkedIn and at events
