# Marketing Intelligence pitch: speaker notes

About 19 minutes across 19 slides. In the deck, press N to show these notes on the slide, or P to open a speaker window with a timer.

## 1. Cover (about 0.5 min)

Marketing Intelligence: one streamlined workflow to reach every member with the right message.

It brings Membership and Marketing & Communications onto one screen. Membership decides who hears from SBF; Marketing & Communications decides how it sounds.

*Let the live wave move for a few seconds before you speak.*

## 2. Background (about 1 min)

SBF's promise rests on two pillars.

Mobilise: bring the right members into the right rooms, through briefings, networks and consultations.

Magnify: make each member's next opportunity visible, through BizSpoke, grants, renewals and member stories.

Both pillars run on member communications. That is where we focus.

## 3. The problem (about 1.5 min)

From 16 department interviews and the member interviews, four problems stand out.

One: no single view of the member. Data is copied into Excel by hand.

Two: events compete and get buried. No shared calendar.

Three: one message for everyone. Members want relevance to their industry and role.

Four: no way to see what worked. Results never return to the member record.

## 4. The solution (about 1 min)

One workflow in five steps, with the member at the centre.

Each problem has a step. Know fixes the single view. Plan fixes the calendar. Create and Approve fix the one-size message. Learn shows what worked.

Sera, our assistant, drafts and recommends. People decide.

## 5. User flow (about 1.5 min)

Six steps: Plan, Create, Audience, Creative, Send, Learn.

Four rows, four stakeholders. Membership plans, approves the audience and acts on next steps.

Sera, highlighted in the middle row, checks, drafts, sends and learns.

Marketing & Communications approves the creative and reviews results.

Members receive, respond, and get the follow-up Learn recommends.

*Trace the arrows left to right with the pointer.*

## 6. 01 · Know (about 1.5 min)

Problem one: no single view. Know is the answer.

Six sources join into one profile: CRM, events, email and WhatsApp, the website, and social signals.

An activity score at a glance, next best actions with the reason shown, and consent and renewal on the same page.

*Scroll the Kopi Lane profile to the next best actions.*

## 7. 02 · Plan (about 1.5 min)

Problem two: events compete. Plan gives every department one calendar.

Sera warns about clashes and send fatigue before they happen.

Each entry takes a custom audience: member groups plus named companies. Add one entry, or import many by CSV.

*Open New Entry and show the audience picker.*

## 8. 03 · Create (about 1.5 min)

Problem three: one message for everyone. Create fixes it.

Sera suggests channels across Email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, and drafts a version for each member group.

Every message links to a landing page, existing or custom, and brand and consent checks run before sign-off.

*Switch the member group and the tone, then open the landing page preview.*

## 9. 03 · Templates (about 1 min)

Eight templates, locked to the brand. Sera changes the words, not the look.

Six for Email, from the BizSpoke Newsletter to the Welcome Letter, plus a LinkedIn Post and a WhatsApp Message.

Each has a default timing, like three weeks before an event.

## 10. 04 · Learn (about 1.5 min)

Problem four: no way to see what worked. Learn closes the loop.

A funnel per campaign, channels and groups compared, and Sera's post-mortem.

Follow-ups such as calling no-shows go straight to the calendar in one click.

*Click Add to calendar on a next action.*

## 11. Work anywhere (about 1 min)

Work anywhere, anytime, without boundaries.

The same home on desktop and phone. Ask SeraAI in plain words, and approve on the move.

Sera suggests. It never sends without both sign-offs.

*Point to the phone where Sera is answering.*

## 12. Roles (about 0.75 min)

Every step has a clear owner: Membership, Sera, Marketing & Communications, and other departments where they add entries or propose audiences.

Nobody waits without knowing whose turn it is.

## 13. Rollout & next steps (about 1.5 min)

Three phases. Phase 1, Engage, ships now: the calendar, AI drafts, fast-tracked sign-off and the activity score.

Phase 2, Understand, adds the full member journey: renewal risk and member matching.

Phase 3, Anticipate, forecasts who may lapse and which events will fill.

Phases 2 and 3 sit on one centralized member database, which also powers the Member Portal: registration, billing, payments, profiling and activities.

Phase 1 connects to the Nexus CRM, the eDM platform, the portals, forms, WhatsApp Business and LinkedIn.

## 14. Live prototype (about 1 min)

This is the system itself, live. Every screen you've seen is this one working prototype.

Start with one quarter and two teams. Thank you.

*Click into the frame and open a group from the sphere, or ask Sera a question.*

## 15. Appendices (about 0.25 min)

Reference slides for questions: the two sign-offs, how to get started, the activity score, and why a native portal.

## 16. A · Approve (about 0.5 min)

Two sign-offs, each team owning its part.

Membership signs off the audience. Marketing & Communications signs off the creative, with the message and landing page side by side.

Routine sends on an unchanged template take the fast track.

## 17. B · Getting started (about 0.5 min)

Start small: one quarter, two teams.

Confirm the pilot data, agree the score and sign-off, then run the quarter and choose the Portal modules together.

## 18. C · Activity score (about 0.5 min)

The score is built from what members do: events, opens, visits, programmes, social and good standing.

Membership sets the weights. Active is 70 and above, Needs Attention below 40.

*Adjust a weight to show it must total 100.*

## 19. D · Why native (about 0.5 min)

Each Portal module gives SBF control of more of the member's life, and each adds a signal that makes the recommendations better.
